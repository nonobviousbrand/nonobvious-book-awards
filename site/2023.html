The 2023
Book Awards

Five winners, ten shortlist selections and a longlist of the year's best non-fiction books — chosen from more than 1,000 entries and announced live in December 2023.

The year's biggest themes: finding the balance between AI and our humanity, redefining good work, celebrating the forgotten foundations of daily life, fixing broken healthcare — and looking beyond happiness itself.

The Biggest Book Trends of 2023

What the year's books
were really about.

As part of our judging process, we curate the biggest trends from all the non-fiction books we evaluate throughout the year. Here are the six biggest trends we identified from books published during 2023.

Trend 01

#HumanAI

A wave of AI books all circled one question: how do we find the balance between humanity and artificial intelligence — and teach the technology to respect the humans it serves.

Trend 02

#GoodWork

Books about work took a deeper turn this year — intrinsic motivation, what a good job really means, and how we create more good jobs in the world.

Trend 03

#ForgottenFoundations

From trees and roads to parking and underwater exploration, these books shined a light on the overlooked foundations of how we live.

Trend 04

#BrokenHealthcare

Why is healthcare broken? These books examined the frayed doctor-patient connection, who pays for what, and where the fixing has to start.

Trend 05

#BeyondHappiness

Instead of chasing happiness itself, these titles reframed what it takes to be happy — through awe, wonder and even awkwardness.

Trend 06

#FixTheFuture

Optimistic books about a future that does not become a dystopia — and what it would actually take to create that future in reality.
