How It Works

An award unlike any other.

There are no predefined entry categories, no entry fees, and no pay-to-win. Every submitted book is actually reviewed by our team, and the best rise to the top — it's that simple.

Phase 01

Submissions

Entries are accepted from mid-August through late October. Authors, publishers and publicists mail two printed copies of each book — there's no entry fee, and no categories to choose.

Phase 02

Judging

Through the fall, our review team actually reads every one of the 1,000+ submissions — selecting the 100-book longlist announced in mid-November, then narrowing it to the shortlist revealed in early December.

Phase 03

Winner Announcements

Each December, five books are named winners — the most Original, Useful, Entertaining, Shareable and Important books of the year — revealed during our live announcement show on YouTube.

2026 Competition

Think a book is a winner?

Entries for the 2026 Non-Obvious Book Awards are now open. Authors, publishers and publicists are all welcome to submit — and it costs nothing to enter.

How To Enter →
  • 1
    No entry fee — ever

    Submitting your book for consideration is completely free.

  • 2
    Two physical copies required

    Mail two printed copies of your book to our submissions address. Galleys and advance copies are accepted.

  • 3
    Print only

    No digital or audio submissions are accepted.

  • 4
    Publication window

    Books must have a publication date between November 2025 and December 2026.
