Best Books Roundups

The Best Books On
Creativity &
Innovation

Non-obvious thinking begins with seeing what others miss. From more than 800 books honored across a decade of the Non-Obvious Book Awards, these are the ones we most recommend on creativity and innovation: where ideas come from, how to champion them inside organizations that resist them, and why the most original thinkers see the world differently.

*This list does not include books published in 2026, which are currently under review as part of our 2026 awards program.

2025 · Longlist

Make Art

by Nishant Jain

The artist behind SneakyArt makes the case that creativity is a practice, not a talent. Drawing the world around you, he argues, changes how you see everything in it.

by Helen Lewis

Journalist Helen Lewis gleefully dismantles the myth of the lone genius, showing how our worship of brilliant individuals shortchanges the teams and conditions that actually produce great work.
