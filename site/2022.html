The 2022
Book Awards

Our most competitive year to that point — five winners, ten shortlist selections and a longlist of the year's best non-fiction books, chosen from more than 1,000 titles and announced live in December 2022.

The year's biggest themes: fighting to save books and bookstores, celebrating underappreciated women, reclaiming the web, learning from animals — and figuring out how to emerge from the pandemic comfortable with ourselves.

The Biggest Book Trends of 2022

What the year's books
were really about.

As part of our judging process, we curate the biggest themes from all the non-fiction books we evaluate throughout the year. Here are the seven biggest themes we identified from books published during 2022.

Trend 01

#savingbooks

As bookstores come under threat, authors explore the evolution of language and passionately defend books and those who sell them.

Trend 02

#underappreciatedwomen

Unsung women in aviation, fitness, entertainment & more are profiled and celebrated along with books that help women to speak up and lead.

Trend 03

#takebacktheweb

The battle for the soul of the Internet is alive and these books define what's at stake and expose the manipulators so we can reclaim the web together.

Trend 04

#talktoanimals

Animals can teach us about ourselves. These titles explore the way animals communicate and think, how we can talk back and why it matters.

Trend 05

#emergefromcovid

As pandemic lockdowns end, the world reckons with what has changed in these books that illuminate the shift and explore how to be happy now.

Trend 06

#decodingbad

Dictators, authoritarians and other bad actors are seemingly winning and these books explore what makes evil triumph and how to fight back.

Trend 07

#comfortablyyou

What does it take to live your best life and be comfortable with yourself? These post-pandemic titles explore how to be ok and maybe even happy.
