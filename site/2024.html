The Biggest Book Trends of 2024

What the year's books
were really about.

As part of our judging process, we curate the biggest trends from all the non-fiction books we evaluate throughout the year. Here are the twelve biggest trends we identified from books published during 2024.

Trend 01

Calming Anxiety & Burnout

From the anxious generation to burned-out workers, books this year offered ways to take back your brain, slow down and find calm in an overstimulated world.

Trend 02

Math Mindedness

A surprising wave of books made the case that math is for everyone — and that loving numbers (or at least not fearing them) can change how you see the world.

Trend 03

Good Aging

Rethinking what it means to get older — from how to be old with style to the golden years of retirement — these books reframed aging as opportunity rather than decline.

Trend 04

Rethinking Higher Ed

Books questioning what education is for, who it serves, and what being educable really means in a world where learning never stops.

Trend 05

Generosity First

Giving to grow, infectious generosity, and the case that leading with kindness and abundance is a strategy — not a sacrifice.

Trend 06

Saving Humanity

As AI accelerates, these books wrestled with what it will do to education, work and our future — and how we keep our humanity at the center.

Trend 07

The Female Lens

Stories and strategies seen through women's eyes — from likeable badasses to negotiating your worth and being seen at work.

Trend 08

Finding Purpose

Is your work worth it? These books asked what makes work — and life — meaningful, and how to find a path that fits.

Trend 09

Climate Solutionism

Beyond doom: books about fixing our waste, rebuilding what we have, and engineering a better world with practical optimism.

Trend 10

The Citizenship Imperative

In an election year, books about grievance, civic life and why participating — from voting to leading — matters more than ever.

Trend 11

Quiet Leadership

The loudest voice isn't the strongest one. These books championed smart-not-loud influence, humility and leading without ego.

Trend 12

The Friendship Antidote

Modern friendship, significant others beyond romance, and the science of connection — the year's cure for an epidemic of loneliness.
